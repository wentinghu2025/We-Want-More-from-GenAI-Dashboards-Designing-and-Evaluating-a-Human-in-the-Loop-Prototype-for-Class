🔍 Student Dialogue Behavior QA

Q: What AI-assisted functions do students generally appreciate when writing lesson plans?

A: Students generally find AI helpful in "polishing" language, enhancing "vividness" of descriptions, and expanding content, which effectively saves writing time.

🤖 AI Literacy & Prompt Engineering

Q: How can teachers use AI prompts to design assessments that challenge students' higher-order thinking?

A: Design assessment prompts (e.g., essay questions) that require students to critically analyze the subject matter, construct logical arguments, and apply their knowledge — thus assessing critical thinking and problem-solving skills.

🤝 Collaborative Problem Solving Behaviors

Q: What student behaviors indicate they have failed to establish common ground? What type of prompt should be used?

A: When students do not proactively express their knowledge or ideas, or do not actively try to understand others' viewpoints, it indicates a failure to establish common ground. Prompts should encourage "establishing shared understanding," such as clarification questions ("What's next?") or paraphrasing a teammate's idea ("Ok, make it heavier").
